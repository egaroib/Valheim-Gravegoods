# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Changed Ashlands and Deep North elemental weapon variants to drop from a separate, much rarer pool (5% of gear drops by default, always quality 1)
- Added the [enhancers] section to loot.txt and the Gear.EnhancedChance setting
- Added DeepNorth as a valid biome in the gravegoods sim usage text

## 0.1.2
- Fixed bonus loot being marked as cheated in chests Valheim 1.0.20 no longer treats as cheated

## 0.1.1
- Changed the required Jotunn version to 2.30.2, matching servers on the latest Jotunn

## 0.1.0
- Added a small chance for world chests to hold biome resources, arrows or bolts, and gear
- Added tiering of gear and ammo from crafting materials, including other mods' items
- Added loot.txt, report.md, and the gravegoods status, sim, report and legacy commands
- Added compatibility with RegeneratingWorld and MultiUserChest
